# The hand: two-plate print, acted

**Two plates.** Colour plate first (`<g class="plates">`, 1.8/1.4 off register): flat fills, halftone-dot shading, cream underlays. Then the pen (`<g class="ink">`): one fine, even #1C2541 line, 3 for outlines and 2.4 for details, slightly wobbly, no hatching. The plates alone must read on a dark card.

**Palette (`palette()`, from the card colour):** cream paper for solids; signal blue #3A7BFF only on the AI, always inside cream with an ink edge (so it reads on blue cards); the person's shirt is the warm ink furthest from the card; the ground shadow is halftone in a darker card tint; blush is pink dots.

**The person leads.** Round head with the curl, bean body, head radius 27 or more so the face reads at 64 px. One emotion: eyes, brows and mouth, plus blush or sweat. The pose has a line of action (`lean`), and the limbs reach something.

**The scene.** One big object, usually the AI, off centre; the person shares the frame; a ground shadow; empty space. No words ("…" and "?" are fine).

**No repeats.** Every card has its own stance and expression. Pen order: person, object, marks. Aim for 11 KB or less.
